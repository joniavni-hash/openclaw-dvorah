# Weekly Summary — Villa Lithos Marketing

**Week of:** 2026-03-20 to 2026-03-27

## Highlights
- Instagram reel with sunset hook reached 820 people — best reach this week
- TikTok video outperformed all other platforms on saves (18)

## What worked
- Visual-emotional hooks (sunset + view) consistently drive higher watch time
- Reels outperform static posts on Instagram

## What didn't work
- Double CTA in mid-post reduced engagement on Facebook
- Carousel without strong opening slide underperformed

## Next week focus
- Test 3 hook variations on top-performing sunset visual
- Move CTA to end only
- Prioritize reels over carousels on Instagram
